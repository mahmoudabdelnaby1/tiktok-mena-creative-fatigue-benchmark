# TikTok Ads Creative Decay Example Methodology

> No campaign dataset was supplied for this repository. All included numerical values are illustrative scenarios, not audited observations or regional estimates.

- **Audited Sample:** None. Do not cite the sample size or infer market benchmarks from these fixtures.
- **Metric Definitions:**
  - *Lifespan:* Days from campaign launch until CPA exceeds 1.5x Day 1 baseline.
  - *Decay Rate:* Percentage decrease in 2-second hook retention rate week-over-week.
